Nuclear test: click a surface to detonate

Positions come from VSOP87/ELP-based ephemerides (Astronomy Engine) and JPL mean elements; orientations follow the IAU rotation models. Earth's relief is real elevation data streamed from the AWS Terrain Tiles; all surfaces continue as GPU fractals below the data resolution, down to centimetres.

Elevation: Terrain Tiles (Mapzen/Tilezen) — SRTM, GMTED2010, NED (USGS), ETOPO1 (NOAA), GEBCO and others. Imagery: NASA Blue Marble & Black Marble. Planetary elevation models and maps: USGS Astrogeology (LRO, Kaguya, MGS, MESSENGER, New Horizons, Cassini, Mars Express, Galileo, Voyager, Clementine), NASA 3D Resources. Shape models: P. Stooke (NASA PDS), Thomas et al. (NASA PDS), Ernst et al. 2023 (CC-BY-4.0), Amalthea map by ItzImcool (CC-BY-4.0), via CelestiaContent. Apophis: radar shape model (Lawrence & Benner, NASA PDS), spin state (Lee et al. 2022), orbit from JPL solution 220 and the JPL Small-Body Database. Stars: HYG via d3-celestial.

Running without the graphics card. The browser draws WebGL in software on the CPU (), so everything is very slow. Turn on hardware acceleration (Chrome / Edge: Settings → System → “Use graphics acceleration when available”, then restart; check chrome://gpu), update the GPU driver, and on laptops with two GPUs let the browser use the high-performance one (Windows: Settings → Display → Graphics).
